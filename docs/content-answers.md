## services

**Residential**
Apartments, villas and independent houses across Bengaluru, for sale and rent. We shortlist against your budget and commute rather than sending you everything on the market.

**Commercial**
Office space, retail units and warehousing, to buy or lease. We shortlist on floor plate, access and parking rather than just rent per square foot.

**Plots & Investments**
Plots in approved layouts and larger land parcels. We verify khata, approvals and title chain before you place any money.

---

## audiences

**First-time homebuyers**
Your first home in Bengaluru, without the guesswork. Budgets, approvals, paperwork and what to expect, explained plainly.

**Investors**
Investors usually get the entry right and the exit wrong. We look at rental yield, appreciation corridors and resale liquidity together, so you're not holding something that only makes sense on paper.

**NRIs**
Most NRI purchases run through a relative doing you a favour, which works until it doesn't. We do live video site visits so you see the property as it is, handle the documentation end to end — approvals, registration, builder and bank follow-ups — and fit calls around your time zone instead of Bengaluru's.

**Tenants**
Renting in Bengaluru usually means the same listing from four brokers and a flat that looks nothing like the photos. We shortlist properly, sort the agreement and deposit terms, and stay involved through move-in.

## about

**paragraphs[0]**
Chetan Vishwanath and Harsha Nanjappa started Zen Crest Realty after watching too many property transactions in Bengaluru go wrong for avoidable reasons — unclear approvals, rushed paperwork, and brokers who disappear once the deal is done. So, we started Zen Crest to give clients the kind of experience we'd want for our own families.

**paragraphs[1]**
A listings portal shows you everything and leaves the filtering to you. We start with a conversation about budget, location, purpose and timeline, then bring you a shortlist we've actually checked — ownership, builder and project details, RERA registration where it applies, and how the asking price sits against the market. We stay involved through site visits, negotiation and closing, coordinating with lawyers, banks and valuers as needed.
